Single Row Subqueries
Write queries to:
Find the employee(s) with the highest salary.
Find the employee(s) with the lowest salary.
Display employees whose salary is greater than the average salary.

 1. Find the employee(s) with the highest salary
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE salary = (SELECT MAX(salary) FROM employees);

 2. Find the employee(s) with the lowest salary
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE salary = (SELECT MIN(salary) FROM employees);

 3. Display employees whose salary is greater than the average salary
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE salary > (SELECT AVG(salary) FROM employees);

Multiple Row Subqueries

Write queries using:
IN
Display employees belonging to departments returned by a subquery.
ANY
Display employees whose salary is greater than ANY salary from the HR department.
ALL
Display employees whose salary is greater than ALL salaries from the Testing department.

 1. IN: Display employees belonging to departments returned by a subquery
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE department IN (SELECT DISTINCT department FROM employees WHERE salary > 60000);

 2. ANY: Display employees whose salary is greater than ANY salary from the HR department
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE salary > ANY (SELECT salary FROM employees WHERE department = 'HR');

 3. ALL: Display employees whose salary is greater than ALL salaries from the Testing department
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE salary > ALL (SELECT salary FROM employees WHERE department = 'Testing');
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
Correlated Subqueries

Write queries to:
Display employees whose salary is greater than the average salary of their own department.
Display employees who receive the highest salary in their respective departments.

 1. Display employees whose salary is greater than the average salary of their own department
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees e
WHERE salary > (
    SELECT AVG(salary)
    FROM employees
    WHERE department = e.department
);

 2. Display employees who receive the highest salary in their respective departments
SELECT emp_id, emp_name, department, salary
FROM employees e
WHERE salary = (
    SELECT MAX(salary)
    FROM employees
    WHERE department = e.department
);
